1. About the Programme

This programme is conducted by Baderia Global Institute of Engineering & Management, Jabalpur, as part of its school outreach activities. It introduces school students to Artificial Intelligence in simple language: what it is, how it is changing our daily life and future careers, and how it can be used in the right way for studies and creative work.

Along with the basic concepts, students are shown some of the most useful AI tools available today, namely Gemini, Gemma, NotebookLM, Nano Banana, Google Flow, Lovable and Google Workspace Studio, through live demonstrations. Safe, honest and responsible use of AI is a part of every module.

2. Course Objectives

  1. To give students a basic understanding of Artificial Intelligence and how it works.
  2. To make students aware of the impact of AI on society, education and careers.
  3. To introduce AI tools that help in learning, creating content, building apps and automating tasks.
  4. To develop the habit of using AI safely, honestly and responsibly.

3. Course Content

Module 1: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

Topics and Concepts
  • Meaning of Artificial Intelligence
  • How machines learn from data: data, training, model and prediction
  • AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning and Generative AI
  • What a Large Language Model (LLM) is, and how a chatbot gives answers
  • Strengths and limitations of AI; AI hallucination (confident but wrong answers)
Examples

YouTube and Instagram recommendations, traffic updates on Google Maps, face unlock on phones, the spam filter in Gmail, Google Lens, voice typing in Hindi, and fraud alerts on UPI payments.

Module 2: Impact of AI on Society and Careers

Topics and Concepts
  • AI in education, healthcare, agriculture and everyday services
  • Changing nature of jobs, and new career options in AI and technology
  • Risks of AI: deepfakes, fake news, misuse of personal data, bias and over-dependence
  • AI initiatives in India
Examples

AI tutors and instant doubt solving; detection of disease from X-rays and eye scans; identifying crop disease from a photo of a leaf; Bhashini for translation into Indian languages; the IndiaAI Mission of the Government of India.

Module 3: Talking to AI – Prompting with Gemini and Gemma

Topics and Concepts
  • What a prompt is, and why a clear prompt gives a better answer
  • The R-T-C-F method of writing prompts: Role, Task, Context and Format
  • Follow-up prompts, and using AI as a tutor instead of copying answers
  • Cloud AI and on-device AI: Gemini (runs on Google’s servers) and Gemma (open model that runs on a laptop or phone, even without internet)
Examples

Comparing a weak prompt (“Tell me about photosynthesis”) with a strong one (“You are my science teacher. Explain photosynthesis to a Class 8 student in five short points with one daily-life example, and then ask me three questions”); asking for step-by-step hints for a maths problem; Gemma answering questions on a phone in airplane mode.

Tools: Gemini, Gemma (through the Google AI Edge Gallery app)

Module 4: AI for Studies – NotebookLM

Topics and Concepts
  • Source-based AI: answers only from the documents you add
  • Citations, and checking every answer against the original text
  • Study guides, summaries, flashcards, quizzes and mind maps
  • Audio Overview (podcast-style summary, also available in Hindi) and Video Overview
  • Limitations, and why personal documents should not be uploaded
Examples

Turning a textbook chapter, such as “Life Processes” from Class 10 Science, into revision notes, flashcards, a quiz and an audio summary.

Tools: NotebookLM

Module 5: AI for Images and Presentations – Nano Banana and Gemini

Topics and Concepts
  • Creating images from a text description (text-to-image)
  • Editing images with simple text instructions
  • Making posters, infographics and presentations with AI
  • Mentioning AI-made content in school work; SynthID, Google’s invisible watermark on AI images
Examples

A “Save Water” campaign poster, which is then edited to show the title in Hindi; a 7-slide presentation on “Natural Disasters in India and How to Stay Safe” drafted in Gemini Canvas and exported to Google Slides.

Tools: Gemini app (Nano Banana and Nano Banana Pro), Gemini Canvas, Google Slides, NotebookLM Slide Deck

Module 6: AI for Video Creation – Google Flow

Topics and Concepts
  • How AI creates video from text and images (Google’s Veo model)
  • Writing a video prompt: subject, action, place, camera movement, lighting and sound
  • Text to Video, Frames to Video, Ingredients to Video and Scene Builder
  • Deepfakes: how to identify them, and why making or sharing them is wrong and punishable
Examples

A short clip of a boat ride through the Marble Rocks at Bhedaghat on the Narmada; a short awareness video for a school project.

Tools: Google Flow

Module 7: Building Apps with AI – Lovable

Topics and Concepts
  • Creating websites and apps by describing them in plain language
  • Improving an app step by step through instructions
  • Why logic, testing and coding still matter when AI writes the code
  • Careers in software and AI engineering
Examples

A quiz website on “Our Solar System” for Class 9 students; other ideas such as a personal portfolio page, a class timetable or a study planner.

Tools: Lovable

Module 8: Automation with AI – Google Workspace Studio

Topics and Concepts
  • Meaning of automation: Trigger → Steps → Result
  • AI agents that work across Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Calendar and Chat
  • Creating an automation by simply describing it in plain language
  • Where automation helps, and where a human decision is still needed
Examples

When a student registers for a science exhibition through a Google Form, the details go into a Sheet and a confirmation email is sent automatically; a weekly summary of emails posted as a to-do list.

Tools: Google Workspace Studio (studio.workspace.google.com)

Module 9: Safe and Responsible Use of AI

Topics and Concepts
  • Checking facts before trusting an AI answer
  • Protecting personal information and privacy
  • Academic honesty: using AI to learn, not to copy
  • Respecting others: no deepfakes, photo morphing or bullying
  • Age limits, guidance of parents and teachers, and reporting cyber crime
Examples

Verifying an AI answer with the textbook; never sharing an Aadhaar number, home address, password or private photos with an AI tool; reporting misuse on the National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 or at cybercrime.gov.in.

4. Course Outcomes

After completing this programme, students will be able to:

  • CO1Explain the basic concepts of AI, Machine Learning and Generative AI, and identify AI in daily life.
  • CO2Discuss the benefits and risks of AI for society, education and careers.
  • CO3Write clear and effective prompts to get useful answers from AI tools.
  • CO4Use AI tools for studying and for creating images, presentations and videos.
  • CO5Understand how apps and automations are built with AI, and why logic and coding still matter.
  • CO6Use AI safely, honestly and responsibly.
